## The climbing day starts on the trail

Tsuzura-iwa is a climbing crag on the Mazukari ridge in Tokyo. Consider it when you want loaded walking and rope work in the same day, with a mountain-training objective. If a trip leaves only one free day, first decide how much of it you want to spend walking and how much on the rock.

[CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/つづら岩、天狗岩/) describes a long-established training crag with an approach of approximately an hour and a half. This is an approach estimate, not the total journey from your Tokyo accommodation. Budget for a mountain outing rather than immediate climbing upon arrival.

## A shared listing does not mean one destination

Both [ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/101) and the publisher list Tsuzura-iwa with Tengu-iwa. They are different walls: Tsuzura-iwa is on the ridge, while Tengu-iwa is beside a waterfall and has a different approach time. Tell partners which crag you intend to visit so that nobody plans around the shorter Tengu-iwa walk.

The publisher identifies a bus from Musashi-Itsukaichi to Senzoku as the access starting point. Check actual services, trails and the start of your climb separately. A bus stop name is not complete navigation. Save an offline map and topo, note the return service, and stop adding objectives once your turnaround time arrives.

## Equipment cannot be inferred from the grade

The description notes that some older pitons have openings that do not accept a carabiner directly and discusses situations requiring slings. This is a clue about older-route equipment, not a guarantee that an anchor is sound or an instruction to copy a technique without assessing it. A competent person must evaluate protection and retreat options.

The same page mentions 山崎ハングルート with a scale of approximately 30 metres. It is an example of the crag's history and lines, rather than this guide's recommended route for a visitor. Select objectives for the whole team, then establish intermediate belays, anchors and descent. If your experience is limited to ground belaying, obtain the necessary skills or suitable instruction first.

## Leave time for walking and retreat

As an editorial planning recommendation, allow room for equipment sorting, the loaded ascent, route finding, descent and the walk out. Carry water, food, rain protection, a headlamp and suitable walking footwear. A headlamp is a contingency, not a reason to climb until dark.

This guide did not verify current access, trail passability or fixed-protection condition at Tsuzura-iwa; obtain recent information before departure and follow notices on site. A lack of new website comments does not prove closure, while a database entry does not guarantee that an approach is passable.

Tsuzura-iwa is a candidate for a purposeful mountain-training day if the team can manage its route and retreat. If the goal is a few quick sport pitches near Tokyo, compare the approach cost with your available time before choosing this ridge crag.
