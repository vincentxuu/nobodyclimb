## Planning this venue

Base Camp Edogawabashi is a useful candidate for a Tokyo climbing day when you want a choice between bouldering and ropes. The [operator’s introduction](https://b-camp.jp/edogawabashi/) lists bouldering, lead climbing and top-rope climbing. Those options require different preparation. This guide uses published information; admission should not be treated as evidence that a visitor has been trained to belay.

## Getting there and allowing time

The operator gives an approximate five-minute walk from Edogawabashi Station and eleven minutes from Kagurazaka Station. These are station-to-gym estimates, so add transfers, finding the exit and check-in to your hotel-to-gym plan. Published opening hours are 12:00–23:00 on weekdays and 10:00–21:00 on weekends and public holidays. There is no fixed weekly closing day, but temporary closures remain possible.

For a visitor with just one available evening, wall availability matters as much as closing time. The home page carries a setting schedule, and a bouldering or rope area can be unavailable while the gym is open. Check your intended area and leave time for registration, changing and warming up.

## Bouldering and ropes need separate plans

The [facilities page](https://b-camp.jp/edogawabashi/facilities/) describes several bouldering areas and roped facilities. If you have shoes but no qualified belay partner, tell reception that you intend to boulder and follow the gym’s briefing. The presence of top ropes does not establish that staff will be available to belay you on demand.

If you want to try ropes without an established partner and the necessary skills, consult the [first-visit information](https://b-camp.jp/edogawabashi/visitor/). Its top-rope experience is one hour for ¥1,100, with a separate facility fee. Participants need to have completed the bouldering introduction or have bouldering experience; check the available session through the operator’s booking arrangements. General admission, a bouldering introduction and the top-rope experience are different services. Budget and book them separately.

## First-visit costs and rental

Checked on 12 October 2026, the [price page](https://b-camp.jp/edogawabashi/charge/) lists tax-inclusive adult full-day admission at ¥2,420 and initial registration at ¥1,100. Together they total ¥3,520 for an ordinary first adult full-day visit, before a top-rope experience or other paid extras.

Regular rental prices are shoes ¥330, chalk ¥110, harness ¥110, an ATC belay device ¥110 and a lead rope ¥550. Initial registration includes free rental of shoes, chalk, a harness and an ATC; the lead rope is not on that free-rental list. Bring socks for rental shoes. Registration is shared across Base Camp branches, so ask reception to check an existing membership rather than assuming you need to pay registration again.

## Three checks before leaving

Decide whether this is a bouldering visit, a rope session with a capable partner or an enquiry about an experience. Check opening information and setting closures for that day. Then list your membership status, rental needs and any experience fee separately so reception can clarify the total.

Renting equipment does not establish competence. Rope-use requirements and eligibility must follow the gym’s instructions. These checks help a short visit start with a clear purpose and reduce the chance of arriving during a closure of your chosen area. Prices and hours are a dated snapshot and need checking again for a future trip.
