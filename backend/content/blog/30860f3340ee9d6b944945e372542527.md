## Who should consider it

Kogashiyama is an outdoor venue around Utsunomiya in Tochigi. [CLIMBING-net's guide](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/古賀志山/) lists bolted leads, multipitch and some top-rope-only routes, identifying the rock as chert. [ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/80) predominantly records face leads. There are objectives for different abilities, but the existence of easier routes is not an invitation to climb independently without outdoor rope experience.

Teams with outdoor leading, belaying and cleaning skills can make this a focused route-selection day. Visitors with gym-only experience should go with a guide who knows the venue. If your Tokyo itinerary has few climbing days, first make sure the whole team accepts the travel and approach commitment. A broad route range does not ensure everyone will immediately find a suitable objective.

## Finding an objective around Fudo Falls

ROCKTOPO organizes photographs and routes under names including Fudo Falls front/left face, right face and far-left face. Its listed popular routes include 競技会ルート at 5.9, ハートブレーカー at 5.10c and 羚羊ハング at 5.11a. Keep the Japanese names when finding a topo so they can be matched on site. Recorded grades are not gear or safety guarantees: check protection, cleaning and whether leading is permitted for each route.

## Landowner conditions come before route counts

CLIMBING-net records conditions including staying out of the Medaki area, not cutting trees, not leaving belongings at the shrine, no fires, carrying out rubbish and wearing a helmet. ROCKTOPO separately says climbing farther left than the far-left sector is prohibited. Do not assume these names describe the same exact boundary. Locate them using a current topo and signs, and remain within a confirmed sector if unsure. Complete current sector boundaries have not been confirmed with landowners or a managing body here.

## Travel, conditions and your climbing day

The publisher uses the Kogashiyama southern trailhead parking area and an approximately 30-minute walk as an approach reference. Its public-transport description includes older place names and a discontinued service, so this guide does not reuse those connections or journey times. Arrange accommodation around Utsunomiya or confirmed transport, agree on the meeting point and return with a local leader, then look up trains. Current transfer and parking charges have not been verified; keep the budget flexible.

Both ROCKTOPO and the publisher provide a year-round season reference, but wet surfaces, recent rain and crowding still require judgment. A suggested day starts with one Fudo Falls sector, confirms routes and anchors, then selects a few objectives the whole team can join. Avoid introducing unfamiliar exercises during cleaning. The [JFA Kogashiyama accident report](https://freeclimb.jp/information/9978/) documents belay confusion involving mock leading and top-rope cleaning; experienced rope climbers should read it before visiting.
