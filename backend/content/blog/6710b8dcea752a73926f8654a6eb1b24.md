## Where it fits in a Tokyo trip

[B-PUMP TOKYO Akihabara](https://pump-climbing.com/gym/akiba/) is a bouldering gym at 1-1-8 Yushima, Bunkyo-ku, Tokyo. Its official site gives about a five-minute walk from either Akihabara or Ochanomizu Station, making it practical before or after city activities. This is a bouldering option. If your original goal was rope leading or belay practice, reset the day's aim to problems, movement and the gym experience.

The official homepage's October 2026 setting news mentions beginner-to-intermediate use on the first floor and a Beginner Wall on the third, alongside other floor and rooftop updates. These are useful first-visit orientation clues: ask staff about current grades and available walls on arrival. A dated setting update is not a permanent problem inventory. Even experienced boulderers should first learn the landing area, movement around the gym and local rules.

## Hours and a first-visit budget

As read on October 12, 2026, Akihabara's official page lists weekday hours of 12:00–23:00, Saturdays and public holidays 11:00–21:00, and Sundays 10:00–21:00, all Japan local time. Check current notices for exceptions and setting. Adult full-day admission is ¥2,420 including tax. Initial registration is ¥2,200, or ¥1,100 for someone registered at another PUMP gym who is new to Akihabara.

Budget example: an adult entirely new to PUMP buying full-day admission pays ¥4,620 for registration plus use. With the other-PUMP registration rate, the total is ¥3,520. These are sums of the two official charges and exclude other spending. The site also lists weekday daytime and evening rates with specific time windows. Check the table for a shorter visit rather than budgeting an evening rate for a full day.

Akihabara lists shoe rental at ¥330, free on the first visit, and says chalk rental is currently unavailable. Powdered chalk requires a chalk bag; liquid chalk may be used on its own. Its [first-visit page](https://pump-climbing.com/gym/akiba/discovery) also asks visitors to bring socks and clothing suitable for movement. Bring suitable chalk supplies or ask beforehand if needed. State your first-visit and PUMP registration status at reception, complete orientation and then warm up.

This budget uses an adult example. Akihabara's official page sets accompaniment, time restrictions and first-visit consent requirements for minors, so an adult itinerary should not simply be reused for children. A guardian should read the branch's rules and prepare the required documents. Group capacity, storage capacity and coaching availability for a specific date have not been verified here; confirm essential arrangements with the gym before booking.

## Leave room in the session

Suggested example: arrive on a weekday afternoon, learn the current grading on easy problems, choose a few interesting movements to work on and finish with energy for dinner. If an outdoor day follows, there is no need to exhaust yourself on every popular problem. This is an editorial plan, not a firsthand crowd assessment or a tested training outcome. If walls are being reset, ask staff to direct you to available areas.

The fees and hours above are the official-page snapshot read on October 12, 2026, not a price guarantee for April 2027. Before travelling, reread Akihabara's own PRICE and OPEN HOUR sections and its latest setting notices. The same check applies when substituting the gym for a rainy outdoor day.
